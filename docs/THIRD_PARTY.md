## Platform logo assets

The SVGs in `assets/logos/` are locally stored source assets for the platform frame generator. `assets/logos/MANIFEST.json` lists the exact source and SHA-256 hash for **each** included file. Most were matched to the recommended PNGs in [PRO100BYTE/console-logos](https://github.com/PRO100BYTE/console-logos) at commit `1de47931607ddf83cbc982d776b68e6cc3864ad7`; the collection declares the MIT license, copied to `assets/logos/LICENSE-MIT.txt`. That license does not grant rights to the console names and logos as trademarks. PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo and Sega marks remain with their respective owners. Use of these marks here does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

The PS2, PS3 and PSP headers use the collection's standalone SVG wordmarks instead of its recommended versions with smaller explanatory text. The selected files are `Sony Playstation 2-30.svg`, `Sony Playstation 3-,09.svg` (the rounded 2009-era PS3 mark) and `Sony PSP-16.svg`; their dark counterparts are also retained as optional variants. The exact per-file paths and hashes are in `assets/logos/MANIFEST.json`. No glyph shapes were redrawn or stretched. Sony's trademarks remain Sony's; the collection's MIT license covers the SVG files, not trademark permission.

The Nintendo Switch 2 white logo and wordmark comes from [Wikimedia Commons, “Nintendo Switch 2 logo transparent.svg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nintendo_Switch_2_logo_transparent.svg), which identifies Nintendo's original artwork as its source and labels the file `PD-textlogo`. Commons also flags it as a trademark. The SVG is placed into the red header without recoloring or changing its proportions. Nintendo retains its trademark rights; the Commons copyright label does not grant trademark permission.

The Mega Drive and Genesis wordmarks are separate regional variants. Both are retained, with Mega Drive selected by default; review the intended region before publication. The Saturn colored variant was omitted because a reliable match to the collection's recommended image was not established. These and other catalog review notes are visible in `logo-preview.html`.

## Game artwork
The retained historical preview (`assets/preview.png`) shows covers of a few games (SnowRunner, Coral Island, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, Fallout 4 and V Rising) only to show what the app produces. That artwork, and the games' names and logos, belong to their publishers and developers. They are not covered by this project's license. If a rights holder objects, the image will be removed.

The completion overlay and default-art sources in `tools/src/` remain available for the Tkinter fallback.

Apart from those images, no game artwork is included. Covers are built on your own machine from the portrait images Steam serves for games in your own library. Generated covers contain publishers' artwork: use them for personal use and do not redistribute them.
